## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-16

### Added
- Initial release of the HiTechCloud eKYC iOS SDK.
- Camera preview with liveness detection.
- Front and back document recognition for 9-digit People's Identity Cards,
  12-digit People's Identity Cards, Citizen Identity Cards, chip-embedded
  Citizen Identity Cards, Electronic Citizen Identity Cards and VNeID QR codes.
- Automatic document detection and capture.
- Image alignment.
- Demo application with face detection and ID card detection screens.
- CocoaPods integration for GoogleMLKit/FaceDetection, TensorFlowLiteSwift and
  OpenCV2.
- Camera, face detector and ID card detector delegates for receiving capture
  results.
